🌿 Full Website Generation Prompt

AESTHETIC DIRECTION: - Organic luxury meets technical precision - Color palette: deep forest green (#2d5a27), warm cream (#f5f0e8), sage (#7a9e6e), gold accent (#c8a96e), almost-black (#0d1a0f) - Typography: Playfair Display (serif, elegant, editorial) paired with DM Sans (clean, modern) - Natural plant motifs — especially monstera leaves — woven through the design as atmospheric texture, NOT decoration - Grain/noise texture overlay across the entire site at ~4% opacity - Light and dark mode support LAYOUT & STRUCTURE: 1. Case Study Modals — full overlay with project description, result stats, smooth scale-in animation 11. Testimonials — 2×2 grid of cards with hover lift, large quote marks in light sage 12. CTA — full-width light section with massive typography, two buttons 15. Footer — dark, logo + links + live clock INTERACTIONS: - Custom cursor: dot + lagging ring, expands to 90px with text label on interactive elements (cursor text changes: "Open", "Explore", "Let's go") - Lerp (linear interpolation) scroll — buttery inertia scroll at 0.08 ease factor - Magnetic buttons — elements lean toward cursor on hover - 3D card tilt on services based on mouse position - Scroll progress bar at top of page in gold - Scroll-triggered counter animations with cubic ease-out - Scroll-triggered timeline line fill - Text scramble on service card titles (hover) - Horizontal drag-to-scroll portfolio with snap points - Dark/light mode toggle (sidebar) - Grain texture animation shifts position every frame TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS: - Single HTML file, no external dependencies - CSS custom properties for all design tokens - prefers-reduced-motion media query support - ::selection styled in sage green - Favicon: monstera leaf emoji SVG - Live clock in footer showing agency timezone - Form with floating labels and animated underline focus states - Canvas particle system on hero (leaf-shaped particles floating upward) - SVG morphing leaf animation using CSS @keyframes on path d attribute - IntersectionObserver for all scroll reveals - requestAnimationFrame for lerp scroll + cursor trail

🖼️ Brand Identity Prompt

Design a complete brand identity system for "Antigravity", a premium digital marketing agency. Brand personality: sophisticated, organic, growth-oriented, trusted. Logo concept: Wordmark combining "Anti" in bold weight and "gravity" in italic serif, with a subtle monstera leaf mark. Should work on both dark and light backgrounds. Color system: - Primary: Forest Green #2d5a27 (trust, growth, depth) - Secondary: Sage #7a9e6e (approachability, nature) - Accent: Gold #c8a96e (premium, achievement) - Background: Warm Cream #f5f0e8 (warmth, texture) - Dark: #0d1a0f (authority, sophistication) Typography: - Display: Playfair Display (editorial authority) - Body: DM Sans (modern clarity) - Mono: DM Mono (data, technical) Brand motif: Monstera deliciosa leaves used as abstract texture and structural elements — symbolizing organic growth, resilience, and reaching toward light. Never kitsch or literal; always refined and atmospheric. Tone of voice: Confident but never arrogant. Poetic but precise. Like a Michelin-starred chef who also understands data science.

⚡ Quick Variation Prompts

Dark mode version: Replace cream #f5f0e8 with #0d1a0f, make text #d4e8cc, surfaces #162118, keep all plant motifs and gold accents. The site should feel like a greenhouse at night. Mobile version: Stack all grid layouts to single column, reduce font sizes by ~20%, keep all animations but reduce complexity. Horizontal scroll portfolio becomes vertical cards. Nav becomes hamburger menu. Minimal version: Strip back to pure typography and white space. No particle canvas, no grain, no plants. Just Playfair + DM Sans, green and cream, editorial grid. Think Kinfolk magazine meets McKinsey. Maximalist version: Add full-bleed photography of plants/nature, video backgrounds, more sections, client logo parade, awards wall, team section with 3D cards.
